UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
DISTRICT OF DELAWARE

Harbor Freight Logistics Inc., Plaintiff,
v.
Northwind Analytics LLC, Defendant.

Case No. 1:26-cv-00417

DEFENDANT'S MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF MOTION TO DISMISS

I. INTRODUCTION
Plaintiff's complaint alleges breach of contract but fails to identify any provision of
the Master Services Agreement that Defendant breached.

II. STATEMENT OF FACTS
The parties entered into the Agreement on March 1, 2026. Plaintiff sent a notice of
breach on June 2, 2026 and filed suit four days later.

III. ARGUMENT
A. Standard of Review
To survive a motion to dismiss, a complaint must state a claim that is plausible on its face.
B. Plaintiff Did Not Allow the Cure Period
1. The Agreement requires fifteen days to cure a material breach before termination.
Plaintiff filed suit four days after its notice, so the cure period had not run.

IV. CONCLUSION
For these reasons, the Court should dismiss the complaint.
