MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026,
between Northwind Analytics LLC ("Provider") and Harbor Freight Logistics Inc. ("Client").

1. DEFINITIONS
"Services" means the data analytics services described in each Statement of Work.
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party.

2. TERM
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, unless
terminated earlier under Section 8.

3. Fees and Payment
3.1 Fees
Client shall pay the fees set out in each Statement of Work.
3.2 Late Payment
Invoices unpaid after thirty (30) days accrue interest at 1% per month.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least
reasonable care.
Section 4.2 shall survive termination of this Agreement for five (5) years.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party's total liability shall exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months
before the claim arose.

8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' written
notice. Either party may terminate immediately if the other party materially breaches
this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days of notice.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF
The parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
